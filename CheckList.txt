FOR Admin Panel Features
The admin panel helps oversee and manage the platform’s operations.

User Management:

Manage customers, delivery partners, and restaurant accounts.
Handle account activation, suspension, and support requests.
Order and Delivery Management:

Monitor all orders in real time.
Handle escalations and refunds.
Revenue Management:

Manage commission rates for restaurants and delivery partners.
Track platform revenue and payouts.
Promotions and Marketing:

Launch promo codes, discounts, and campaigns.
Send notifications or emails to users.
Analytics and Reporting:

Track platform performance metrics: order volume, active users, and popular restaurants.
Analyze customer trends and delivery efficiency.
Content Moderation:

Approve/reject restaurant listings, menu items, and reviews.

Can include chat features
